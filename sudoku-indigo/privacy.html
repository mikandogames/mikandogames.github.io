Sudoku Indigo ("the app") is published by MiKando (Mert Kandemir) ("we"). Contact: mertkandemir01@gmail.com

Data we collect. We do not collect personal data ourselves. There are no accounts. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, and short full-screen ads between levels or puzzles. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking; you can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
