Frequently asked

My progress is gone after reinstalling. Progress is stored only on your device, so uninstalling the app deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the game, go to Settings and tap Privacy choices (shown in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland). On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

A video did not give me the reward. The reward is given when the video finishes. If no video was available, the reward is given for free.
